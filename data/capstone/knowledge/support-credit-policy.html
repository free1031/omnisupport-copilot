Service credit and refund control policy

Policy owner: Customer Operations and Finance. Classification: internal.

Agent authority

A support agent may document impact and propose a service credit. The agent may not execute a refund. Credits at or above USD 500, any refund, and any externally visible financial adjustment require approval from support operations or billing operations.

Required evidence

The request must contain ticket_id, affected service window, verified impact, amount, currency, business reason, evidence identifiers, idempotency key, actor, and trace_id. Missing evidence is a denial, not a prompt for the model to invent context.

Execution

The controlled tool writes a checkpoint first. Approval resumes the stored payload; it must not reconstruct financial arguments from chat. Reusing the same idempotency key with different arguments is rejected.

Audit

Store approval_id, reviewer, decision reason, action lineage, release_id, and immutable audit event. A rejected request remains visible for review.
