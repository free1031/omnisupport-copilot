Northstar Studio scheduled job recovery

Applies to Studio 1.4 and 1.5. Error codes: ST-SCHED-005, ST-EXEC-002, ST-MONITOR-003.

Duplicate schedule execution

A timezone transition or an outdated schedule revision may cause a job to appear twice. Before rerunning, compare run_id, scheduled_for, workflow revision, and idempotency key.

Recovery procedure

  1. Pause the schedule without deleting it.
  2. Identify the canonical run using the earliest accepted idempotency key.
  3. Cancel only duplicate pending runs; do not terminate a completed business transaction.
  4. Set the schedule timezone explicitly and publish a new schedule revision.
  5. Run one canary execution and verify downstream row counts and evidence.

Backfill

A backfill is a controlled window, not an unrestricted rerun. Record window_start, window_end, source checkpoint, expected partitions, operator, and release_id. Compare completeness after execution.

Safety

When a workflow can send notifications or write external systems, backfill requires human approval and a destination allowlist.
